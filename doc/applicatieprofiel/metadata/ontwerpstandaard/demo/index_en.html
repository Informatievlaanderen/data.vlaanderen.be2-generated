Entiteiten

Filing

Beschrijving
Activity in which a Financial Report is officially submitted.
Gebruik
It is typically a legal obligation, e.g. the filing of annual accounts with the National Bank of Belgium, intended to provide insight into the financial position of the Organization with which one is dealing.
Eigenschappen
Voor deze entiteit zijn de volgende eigenschappen gedefinieerd: date , DepositedBy .
Eigenschap Verwacht Type Kardinaliteit Beschrijving Gebruik Codelijst
date Moment 1 The date on which the report was filed.
DepositedBy Organization 1 The Organization to which the report was filed

Organization

Beschrijving
A number of people organized into a community or other social, commercial, or political structure. The group has a common goal or raison d'être that transcends its individual members and can act as an agent. An organization often has a hierarchical structure.
Eigenschappen
Voor deze entiteit zijn de volgende eigenschappen gedefinieerd: preferred name .
Extra informatie
[object Object]
Eigenschap Verwacht Type Kardinaliteit Beschrijving Gebruik Codelijst
preferred name String 1 Name with which the organization is preferably indicated.
