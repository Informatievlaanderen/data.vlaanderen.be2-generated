This document describes an application profile, in this case Application profile Room reservation. The application profile specifies a semantic data model covering a set of usecases. The specification consists of terms with their constraints (cardinalities, codelists, usage guidelines).

Summary

The OSLO Room Reservation application profile provides a detailed framework for describing room reservations and the associated data, using an appropriate vocabulary, enriched with terms from other vocabularies.

The core of this profile is the reservation, which functions as a central connection between public services (see OSLO Service Catalogue) and infrastructure (see OSLO-Culture and Youth Infrastructure).

A reservation is recorded by an Agent (see OSLO-Generic) for a specific Activity (see OSLO-Cultural Participation), within a defined period and with the addition of a buffer period and location information. Each reservation has a status such as request, pending, or approved, depending on the progress of the processing.

This infrastructure reservation is part of the public service, with a public organisation as responsible (see OSLO Organisation). This organization has attributes such as area of operation, contact information, and preferred name, and acts as the central administrator of the infrastructure.

Infrastructure has different types of attributes. There is one mandatory attribute, which is the location, because every piece of infrastructure must have at least one location. Other attributes such as cancellation policy, accessibility, availability, ownership, opening and accessibility can be optionally shared and are not always required.

Furthermore, there are elements that do not have to be present, but may occur several times. These include documents associated with the infrastructure, as well as capacity, contact, dimension, facility, usage, name, access, infrastructure type, and security requirements.

An important feature of infrastructure is that it can consist of both indoor spaces and outdoor spaces, such as indoor halls or terraces. This provides flexibility in designing the infrastructure for various types of events and activities.

Infrastructure reservation

Description

Subclass of
Reservation
Properties
For this entity the following properties are defined: Buffer , Infrastructure , Location Description , Period of project , Reserved by , reserved for , Status .
Property Expected Range Cardinality Description Usage Codelist
Buffer Period of project 0..* Period that creates time or space for a process or action. In connection with room reservation, a "buffer" is used to prevent two events or meetings from being scheduled immediately after each other in the same room. It is a fixed time span that cannot be reserved after a room reservation. For example, if a room is booked between 10 am and 5 pm and it can only be booked once a day, the buffer is 8 am to 10 am and 5 pm to 6 pm (opening hours of the room).

Public Organization

Description
An Organisation that belongs to the public sector according to a legal framework, regardless of the level of government at which that framework is in force.
Usage
The public and semi-public organisations that can be classified as public sector are referred to in European Directive 2003/98/EC as "public bodies" and defined as: "the State, regional or local authorities, bodies governed by public law, associations formed by one or more of such bodies or one or more of such bodies governed by public law". And a body governed by public law means 'any body which (a) has been established for the specific purpose of meeting needs in the general interest, not having an industrial or commercial character, and (b) has legal personality, and (c) the activities of which are financed, for the most part, by the State, regional or local authorities or by other bodies governed by public law, either the management is subject to supervision by the latter, or more than half of the members of the administrative, managerial or supervisory board are appointed by the State, regional or local authorities or other bodies governed by public law.';
