Authority

URI
https://productencatalogus.data.vlaanderen.be/ns/ipdc-lpdc#InstancePublicService
Definition
Institutions are the actual interpretation of a product or service. The competent authority can vary: federal, Flemish, provincial,... And the service can be provided by another government such as a local government. A government service is a mandatory or optional set of activities that are or may be carried out by or on behalf of a public organisation, publicly funded and arising from public policy. Services may benefit an individual, an organisation or association, a company or other public authority, or groups of any of these parties. A service exists regardless of whether it is used or not. According to the revised European Interoperability Framework, a European public service includes any service provided by public authorities in Europe, or by other organisations on their behalf, to businesses, citizens or other public authorities.

Concept

URI
https://productencatalogus.data.vlaanderen.be/ns/ipdc-lpdc#ConceptualPublicService
Definition
The services provided by local authorities are often based on Flemish, federal or European legislation, which is translated to a local level. This means that the basis is the same for every local government, but that there may be flexibility for the local interpretation. Because we want to get and keep IPDC well stocked and up to date, with a limited administrative burden, we provide the concept. A draft is a template that editors of the Flemish government prepare for local authorities. It contains a general interpretation of a government service, which can also be applicable in most local contexts. Local authorities can consult these concepts and decide to register their own services on the basis of a concept. At that moment, a concept is cloned to an authority. The local government can then fully adapt this body and tailor it to the local context. In this way, a concept also groups together all the different local variations of a similar service.

Condition

URI
http://data.europa.eu/m8g/Requirement
Definition
A normal requirement is an atomic requirement. Some criteria can be expressed with several atomic requirements. A normal requirement can specify the expected value that the requirement response has to contain, or a range of threshold values within which the requirement response has to fit in. The normal requirement may apply to a certain period of time. It also can provide a list of candidate evidences that the responder can use to prove the normal requirement.

Procedure

URI
http://purl.org/vocab/cpsv#Rule
Definition
The Rule class represents a document that sets out the specific rules, guidelines or procedures that the Public Service follows. It includes the terms of service, licence, and authentication requirements of the Public Service. Instances of the Rule class are FRBR (Functional Requirements for Bibliographic Records) Expressions, that is, a concrete expression such as a document, of the more abstract concept of the rules themselves. This model does not envisage instances of the Rule class as machine-readable business rules. Detailed modelling of the rules related to Public Services is out of scope of this model.
