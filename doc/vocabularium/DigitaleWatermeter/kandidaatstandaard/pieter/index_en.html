This document describes a vocabulary, in this case Vocabulary Utilities Meters. A vocabulary is a collection of reusable terms, namely classes and properties, whose meaning is recorded through a label and definition. For terms defined in this domain, additional formal agreements are available. For external terms, the formal agreements are copied from the external source.

Summary

The vocabulary "OSLO Digital Water Meter" defines terms specifically for the management and monitoring of water consumption via digital water meters. It describes characteristic features of digital water meters, including components such as consumption monitoring, sensors, and meters. These terms have been adapted to accurately describe (drinking) water consumption. This vocabulary is aligned with OSLO models IMKL (Information Model Cables and Pipes), tailored to the specific requirements and functionalities of digital water meters.

6. External terminology

Since 2025, external terms are no longer included as non-normative mapping in vocabularies of data.vlaanderen.be.

Instead, a table with which external URIs are used has been added to each application profile. This simplifies the objective of this document. Namely listing and managing terms defined in this namespace.

To still achieve harmonization of the use of external terminology, a general mapping is published.
