Registered Person

URI https://data.vlaanderen.be/ns/persoon#GeregistreerdPersoon
Definition Person whose data is registered in a register.
Label Registered Person
Usage note Usually this register is a population register, but it can also be an electoral register, for example. The registered data relate to the identity (e.g. surname and first name) and the place of residence of the person and to important life events such as birth, marriage, death, etc. These data are typically recorded by the government, they provide legal protection to the registered person and allow the government to compile basic statistics on its population.
