This document describes a vocabulary, in this case Mobility: Trips & Offers. A vocabulary is a collection of reusable terms, namely classes and properties, whose meaning is recorded through a label and definition. For terms defined in this domain, additional formal agreements are available. For external terms, the formal agreements are copied from the external source.

Summary

The vocabulary "OSLO-Mobility: Trips and Offerings" defines terms that apply to (1) trips made by individuals and (2) the mobility services available to them for this purpose.

Of the trips, things are defined as the route followed, the services/resources/infrastructure used, the operator, the tickets purchased, passenger preferences and characteristics.

Mobility services include concepts such as the resources to be reserved by the passenger, the infrastructure available to the service, details of the provider and the operator, and the licences they hold.

6. External terminology

Since 2025, external terms are no longer included as non-normative mapping in vocabularies of data.vlaanderen.be.

Instead, a table with which external URIs are used has been added to each application profile. This simplifies the objective of this document. Namely listing and managing terms defined in this namespace.

To still achieve harmonization of the use of external terminology, a general mapping is published.
