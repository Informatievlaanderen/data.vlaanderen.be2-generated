This document describes a vocabulary, in this case IPDC - LPDC (Vocabulary). A vocabulary is a collection of reusable terms, namely classes and properties, whose meaning is recorded through a label and definition. For terms defined in this domain, additional formal agreements are available. For external terms, the formal agreements are copied from the external source.

Summary

This implementation model is an implementation model of the ICEG application profile public service and follows the OSLO Vocabulary 'Service'. Many things are taken from the ICEG model and are therefore only described in English. Specific extensions within this data model are described in Dutch.

This implementation model is used to exchange data on public services between IPDC (version 3) and LPDC. It describes how to communicate to and from the local product and service catalogue (LPDC) and the inter-administrative product and service catalogue (IPDC).

Want to know more?

A complete overview of all available concepts and instances can be found in the product catalogue. Behind the scenes, all these services are stored in IPDC. If you wish to integrate the information about this service into your own solutions, this is possible via the IPDC API. Be sure to take a look at the connection guide.

4. Classes

This section provides a formal definition to each class.

Class Authority

Type Class
URI https://productencatalogus.data.vlaanderen.be/ns/ipdc-lpdc#InstancePublicService
Specialization of
  • http://purl.org/vocab/cpsv#PublicService
Definition Institutions are the actual interpretation of a product or service. The competent authority can vary: federal, Flemish, provincial,... And the service can be provided by another government such as a local government. A government service is a mandatory or optional set of activities that are or may be carried out by or on behalf of a public organisation, publicly funded and arising from public policy. Services may benefit an individual, an organisation or association, a company or other public authority, or groups of any of these parties. A service exists regardless of whether it is used or not. According to the revised European Interoperability Framework, a European public service includes any service provided by public authorities in Europe, or by other organisations on their behalf, to businesses, citizens or other public authorities.

Class Concept

Type Class
URI https://productencatalogus.data.vlaanderen.be/ns/ipdc-lpdc#ConceptualPublicService
Definition The services provided by local authorities are often based on Flemish, federal or European legislation, which is translated to a local level. This means that the basis is the same for every local government, but that there may be flexibility for the local interpretation. Because we want to get and keep IPDC well stocked and up to date, with a limited administrative burden, we provide the concept. A draft is a template that editors of the Flemish government prepare for local authorities. It contains a general interpretation of a government service, which can also be applicable in most local contexts. Local authorities can consult these concepts and decide to register their own services on the basis of a concept. At that moment, a concept is cloned to an authority. The local government can then fully adapt this body and tailor it to the local context. In this way, a concept also groups together all the different local variations of a similar service.

Usage

 A good example of a concept is marriage. Although the description and conditions of a marriage are largely the same for the different municipalities, there may still be local differences, such as the location and price, which can be found in the respective authorities.

Class ConceptSnapshot

Type Class
URI https://productencatalogus.data.vlaanderen.be/ns/ipdc-lpdc#ConceptualPublicServiceSnapshot
Definition A concept snapshot depicts what a concept looked like at a certain point in time. Each time a functional change is made, a new concept snapshot will be created. In this way, the complete editorial history of a concept is recorded.

Usage

 The concept snapshots are made available by IPDC on the basis of a linked data event stream (LDES). This LDES makes the full history of all concept changes available to connectors. More info about LDES is available here: https://semiceu.github.io/LinkedDataEventStreams

6. External terminology

Since 2025, external terms are no longer included as non-normative mapping in vocabularies of data.vlaanderen.be.

Instead, a table with which external URIs are used has been added to each application profile. This simplifies the objective of this document. Namely listing and managing terms defined in this namespace.

To still achieve harmonization of the use of external terminology, a general mapping is published.
