Authority

URI https://productencatalogus.data.vlaanderen.be/ns/ipdc-lpdc#InstancePublicService
Definition Institutions are the actual interpretation of a product or service. The competent authority can vary: federal, Flemish, provincial,... And the service can be provided by another government such as a local government. A government service is a mandatory or optional set of activities that are or may be carried out by or on behalf of a public organisation, publicly funded and arising from public policy. Services may benefit an individual, an organisation or association, a company or other public authority, or groups of any of these parties. A service exists regardless of whether it is used or not. According to the revised European Interoperability Framework, a European public service includes any service provided by public authorities in Europe, or by other organisations on their behalf, to businesses, citizens or other public authorities.
Label Authority

Concept

URI https://productencatalogus.data.vlaanderen.be/ns/ipdc-lpdc#ConceptualPublicService
Definition The services provided by local authorities are often based on Flemish, federal or European legislation, which is translated to a local level. This means that the basis is the same for every local government, but that there may be flexibility for the local interpretation. Because we want to get and keep IPDC well stocked and up to date, with a limited administrative burden, we provide the concept. A draft is a template that editors of the Flemish government prepare for local authorities. It contains a general interpretation of a government service, which can also be applicable in most local contexts. Local authorities can consult these concepts and decide to register their own services on the basis of a concept. At that moment, a concept is cloned to an authority. The local government can then fully adapt this body and tailor it to the local context. In this way, a concept also groups together all the different local variations of a similar service.
Label Concept
Usage note A good example of a concept is marriage. Although the description and conditions of a marriage are largely the same for the different municipalities, there may still be local differences, such as the location and price, which can be found in the respective authorities.

Definition A concept snapshot depicts what a concept looked like at a certain point in time. Each time a functional change is made, a new concept snapshot will be created. In this way, the complete editorial history of a concept is recorded.
Label ConceptSnapshot
Usage note The concept snapshots are made available by IPDC on the basis of a linked data event stream (LDES). This LDES makes the full history of all concept changes available to connectors. More info about LDES is available here: https://semiceu.github.io/LinkedDataEventStreams

Condition

URI http://data.europa.eu/m8g/Requirement
Definition A normal requirement is an atomic requirement. Some criteria can be expressed with several atomic requirements. A normal requirement can specify the expected value that the requirement response has to contain, or a range of threshold values within which the requirement response has to fit in. The normal requirement may apply to a certain period of time. It also can provide a list of candidate evidences that the responder can use to prove the normal requirement.
Label Condition
Usage note Not all public services are needed or usable by everyone. For example, the visa service operated by European countries is not needed by European citizens but is needed by some citizens from elsewhere, or public services offering unemployment benefits and grants are targeting specific societal groups. This model reuses the Core Criterion and Core Evidence Vocabulary for this class. The CCCEV provides more details but the Requirement class has three mandatory properties.

URI http://purl.org/vocab/cpsv#Rule
Definition The Rule class represents a document that sets out the specific rules, guidelines or procedures that the Public Service follows. It includes the terms of service, licence, and authentication requirements of the Public Service. Instances of the Rule class are FRBR (Functional Requirements for Bibliographic Records) Expressions, that is, a concrete expression such as a document, of the more abstract concept of the rules themselves. This model does not envisage instances of the Rule class as machine-readable business rules. Detailed modelling of the rules related to Public Services is out of scope of this model.

The categories and subcategories were determined by the European Parliament. These were translated by the IPDC - LPDC team into a code list in the absence of a European code list.
Label yourEuropeCategories
UsageNote The valid values can be found in the ttl file on the link in the code list column.
