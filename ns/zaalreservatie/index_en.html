This document describes a vocabulary, in this case Vocabulary Room Reservation. A vocabulary is a collection of reusable terms, namely classes and properties, whose meaning is recorded through a label and definition. For terms defined in this domain, additional formal agreements are available. For external terms, the formal agreements are copied from the external source.

Summary

The vocabulary OSLO-Room Reservation defines terms specific to the domain of room reservations, with a focus on unique classes and properties that support this context. The classes Infrastructure Reservation, Infrastructure Reservation Service, and Infrastructure Reservation Status, which together form the basis for describing the reservation processes and the associated services.

The Infrastructure Reservation Service provides detailed descriptions of the services and procedures required to manage such reservations, including while the Infrastructure Reservation Status is used to track the progress of a reservation, using terms such as request, pending, and approved.

To determine the specific location and circumstances of a reservation, the vocabulary uses the class Inventory of Fixtures. This class provides a comprehensive description of the physical location that is reserved, such as the specific room or the characteristics of the space.

The vocabulary also includes a unique feature, Vehicle Type, which allows for the inclusion of specific requirements or restrictions regarding transport and access in the context of the reservation.

In addition, there are properties such as Cancellation Policy and Attachment that provide additional context for the conditions under which things like cancellations can take place, and what documents may be involved.

Unique properties such as Buffer and Reserved for help specify the preparation time and the specific activities for which the reservation was made. This creates clarity and conforms the reservation to the intended context of use.

The vocabulary provides a coherent framework that is specially included in terms that are within the OSLO Room Reservation domain and are not dependent on other OSLO processes.

A detailed explanation and application of these terms within the context of room reservations can be found in the OSLO Room Reservation application profile.

5. Properties

Property Buffer

Type Property
URI https://data.vlaanderen.be/ns/zaalreservatie#buffer
Domain https://data.vlaanderen.be/ns/zaalreservatie#Infrastructuurreservatie
Range http://data.europa.eu/m8g/PeriodOfTime
Definition Period that creates time or space for a process or action.
Usage In connection with room reservation, a "buffer" is used to prevent two events or meetings from being scheduled immediately after each other in the same room. It is a fixed time span that cannot be reserved after a room reservation.

6. External terminology

Since 2025, external terms are no longer included as non-normative mapping in vocabularies of data.vlaanderen.be.

Instead, a table with which external URIs are used has been added to each application profile. This simplifies the objective of this document. Namely listing and managing terms defined in this namespace.

To still achieve harmonization of the use of external terminology, a general mapping is published.
